# The Pomodoro

The pane under the outline is a timer, drawn as a small world that changes with your theme: a forest with rabbits, a tower on a hill, snowy peaks, a castle with bats, the great wave, a neon horizon, a rainbow…

## How it tells the time

- `F2` starts a twenty-five minute **writing** session, and pauses and resumes it. `F3` resets it.
- **The sun's position is the clock.** It crosses the sky over the session, so you read the time left from the light instead of watching numbers, and the ground fills in behind it as it goes. The pane's title shows the time too: `writing · 18:04`.
- A **paused** sun waits, dimmed.
- **Break time is night**: five minutes of moon and stars, and whatever comes out at night in that world.

`F2` and `F3` work from anywhere. With the Pomodoro pane itself selected (`Tab` to it), `Enter` or `Space` starts and pauses, and `r` resets.

## The other view

`←` and `→` (or `h` and `l`) in the pane switch between the **Pomodoro** and the **Visualizer**; the pane's bottom edge shows which one you're on.

A sprint (see *Sprints and targets*) runs on the same timer.
