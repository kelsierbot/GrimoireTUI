# Find & replace

## In this scene

`Ctrl-F` (or *Find in this scene* in `Ctrl-K`) opens a find bar under the prose. Matches light up as you type.

- `Enter` or `↓` goes to the next match, `↑` to the one before.
- `Tab` moves to the **replace** box. `Enter` there replaces the current match; `Ctrl-R` replaces every match in the scene.
- `Esc` closes the bar.

## In the whole book

`Ctrl-F` a second time (or `/` in the outline) searches every scene and note, grouped by part and chapter.

- `↑` `↓` choose a match and `Enter` goes to it.
- `Tab` moves to the replace box. `Enter` there (or `Ctrl-R`) asks before replacing across the book: `y` to go ahead, anything else to cancel.

## Whole words and capitals

Find matches **whole words in their exact case** unless you say otherwise, so replacing "Ann" never touches "planned".

- `Ctrl-W` (or `Alt-W`) switches between whole words and inside words.
- `Ctrl-E` (or `Alt-C`) switches between exact case and any case.

The bar shows which is on.

## Undoing a replace

A replace across the whole book is **one step**: `Ctrl-Z` puts every scene back (from the outline, or from the page if you haven't typed since). Each scene's previous version also goes to its history first.
