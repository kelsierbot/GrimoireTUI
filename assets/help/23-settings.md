# Settings

*Esc › Settings…* opens a menu of its own. Switches stay open to show their new state; `←` or `Esc` goes back to the main menu.

- **Themes…**: the nineteen themes and your own (see *Themes*). Also `F9`.
- **Music source…**: YouTube Music, Spotify, Jellyfin or Plex.
- **Turn music on / off**: music is off until you turn it on.
- **Turn spellcheck on / off**.
- **Turn tree icons on / off**: a small symbol beside each row of the outline.
- **Line width**: how wide the prose runs — 60, 72, 80 or 100 columns, or the whole pane.
- **Turn typewriter scrolling on / off**: in focus mode, keep the line you're writing mid-screen (see *Focus mode*).
- **Manuscript look…**: the submission manuscript's format (see *Compile*).
- **Author details…**: your name and contact block for the title page.

Settings are remembered in `~/.config/grimoire/`, and the manuscript's look and your author details are kept in the book's `novel.toml`, so they travel with it.
