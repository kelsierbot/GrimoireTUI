# Themes

`F9` anywhere, or `t` in the outline, opens the themes. There are nineteen, from Grimoire and Lost Forest to Nord, Dracula, Kanagawa, Synthwave '84 and Rainbow — each with its own colours, its own Pomodoro world and its own Visualizer.

- `↑` `↓` (or `j` `k`) **preview each theme live** as you move, so you choose by looking.
- `Enter` keeps it; `Esc` puts back what you had.

Every theme is dark: Grimoire draws on your terminal's own background.

## Rainbow

Everything the other themes draw in their main colour — the focused border, titles, the progress bar — is a spectrum that flows gently round the colour wheel, once every twelve seconds.

## A theme of your own

The last row, **Custom…**, opens a swatch editor.

- Twelve colours, each shown with its hex code. `↑` `↓` move between them; type six hex digits and the colour changes as the sixth lands; `Enter` moves on.
- Below the colours, **pomodoro** and **visualizer** borrow any theme's world and any theme's Visualizer — Kanagawa's wave with Synthwave's grid, say. `←` `→` cycle them, previewed in the pane as you go.
- `Esc` saves and closes.

Custom starts from whichever theme you were looking at, so pick the closest one and adjust.

Your choice is remembered in `~/.config/grimoire/theme.toml`.
