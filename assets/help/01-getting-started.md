# Getting started

Grimoire is a writing desk for one book at a time. The book is a folder of plain Markdown files, so everything you write can be read, copied and backed up without Grimoire.

## The desk

- **The outline**, on the left, headed by the book's title: parts, chapters and scenes, then the notebook (Characters, Places, Notes, Research) and the rest of the book's sections.
- **The page**, on the right: the scene you're writing.
- **The Pomodoro**, under the outline: a timer drawn as a little world. `←` `→` turns it into the Visualizer.
- **Music**, below that, when you've turned it on.
- **The status bar**, along the bottom: the book's words against its target, what you've written today, messages, and the keys that work where you are.

## Getting around

- `Tab` and `Shift-Tab` move between the panes.
- `Esc` opens **the menu**, from anywhere. Everything Grimoire can do is in it, down to *Quit Grimoire* at the bottom. `Esc` again closes it and leaves you where you were.
- `Ctrl-K` **finds anything**: every action, scene, note and theme by a few letters of its name, with the key that does the same thing.
- `?` or `F1` opens this help, on the topic for wherever you are. On the page, and in any box you type into, `?` is just a question mark, so use `F1` there.

Where your terminal can send it (Ghostty, Kitty, WezTerm, foot), `Cmd` works everywhere `Ctrl` does. The status bar shows whichever yours delivers.

## Saving and quitting

You never have to save. A scene is written two seconds after you stop typing, and never waits longer than twenty seconds. `Ctrl-S` saves at once if you like.

To quit: `Ctrl-Q`, `q` in the outline, or *Quit Grimoire* at the bottom of the menu. Everything is saved first. If a save can't happen (a full disk, say), Grimoire stays open and says so; `Ctrl-Q` again leaves anyway, and the words wait in recovery for next time.

## The mouse

Click a pane to move to it, a chapter to fold it, a scene to open it, and the prose to put the cursor there. Drag across the prose to select. The wheel scrolls whatever is under the pointer.

## About Grimoire

*About Grimoire…* in the menu (or `Ctrl-K` › *About Grimoire*) shows which version you're running and who made it: Grimoire is made by Catfinity Studios. `Enter`, or a click on the link, opens catfinity.com in your browser, and `l` opens the License topic. Catfinity Studios also makes **Catfinity**, an idle cat RPG for iPhone and Android: `c` in *About Grimoire…* (or a click on its line) opens its page. From a terminal, `grimoire --version` says the same.

## Donate

Grimoire is free, and a donation is never required. It's always appreciated, though, and it all goes toward Grimoire's development. *Donate…* in the menu (or `Ctrl-K` › *Donate (Ko-fi)*, or `d` in *About Grimoire…*) opens the Ko-fi page in your browser.
