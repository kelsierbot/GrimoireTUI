---
title: "Novel Format"
---

How this book is laid out. Every section folds; `Enter` or `Space` opens and closes one.

**Manuscript** is the book itself: pages hold chapters, chapters hold scenes. `p` makes a page, `c` a chapter, `n` a scene. Only what's in here counts toward your word count and goes into an export.

**Characters** and **Places** are your notebook. Their names teach the spellchecker, and `Ctrl-O` in a scene opens the note for a name under the cursor.

**Front Matter** holds the pages before chapter one, one folder per edition: Manuscript Format for a Word submission, Ebook for the EPUB, Paperback for print. Starter pages are set to `compile: false`; change that to `true` when one is ready to go into the book.

**Notes** and **Research** are for everything else you gather. **Sample Output** is a place to keep exports you want to compare.

**Template Sheets** are blank forms. Copy one into Characters or Places to fill in.

**Trash** keeps whatever you delete until you delete it from there too.
