The building had no windows on the north face, which was the first thing that struck her as wrong. Every other structure on the ridge had been designed to catch the morning light. This one turned its back on it.

She checked the address again. Correct. Of course it was correct — she had written it down twice, once in the car and once standing here in the gravel, as if the second writing might reveal a different building.
